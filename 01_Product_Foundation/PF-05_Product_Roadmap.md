# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the long-term product evolution strategy and release roadmap for Digitize by OreeOn Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

Rather than viewing the platform as a collection of independent releases, this document establishes a structured evolution plan that aligns business objectives, product capabilities, engineering priorities, and commercial strategy.

It provides a single source of truth for what will be delivered in each phase, why those capabilities are being introduced, and how each release contributes to the overall vision of becoming the operating platform for the global activity and recreation industry.

This document serves as the strategic reference for founders, product managers, architects, engineering teams, quality assurance, sales, customer success, and implementation teams.

---

# 2. Executive Summary

Digitize by OreeOn Technologies Pvt. Ltd. has been designed as a configurable SaaS platform that will evolve through carefully planned release phases.

The platform will not attempt to solve every business problem in its initial release.

Instead, each phase introduces a focused set of capabilities that build upon a stable business architecture established during Phase 1.

This approach provides several advantages:

* Faster time-to-market.
* Lower implementation risk.
* Better customer feedback.
* Incremental business value.
* Predictable product evolution.
* Sustainable engineering practices.

Each release has clearly defined business objectives, functional boundaries, and measurable success criteria.

Future capabilities will extend the platform through configuration rather than architectural redesign.

---

# 3. Product Release Philosophy

The release strategy is governed by five core principles.

---

## Principle 1 — Foundation Before Expansion

A stable operational foundation must be established before introducing additional business domains or advanced capabilities.

The first release focuses on delivering operational excellence for swimming pools.

Only after validating the platform architecture and customer adoption will new Facility Types be introduced.

---

## Principle 2 — Validate Before Scaling

Every major capability should be validated through real customer usage before expanding the product scope.

Customer feedback drives future investment priorities.

Platform maturity takes precedence over feature quantity.

---

## Principle 3 — Configuration Before Customization

New industries should be supported by extending platform configuration rather than creating independent products or customer-specific customizations.

This principle protects the long-term maintainability of the platform.

---

## Principle 4 — Business Value First

Every release must deliver measurable business value.

Features that do not improve customer operations, operational efficiency, or commercial growth should not be prioritized.

Business outcomes take precedence over feature volume.

---

## Principle 5 — Continuous Evolution

Digitize by OreeOn is designed to evolve continuously.

Each release should enhance the platform while preserving compatibility with existing customers.

Growth should occur through incremental improvements rather than disruptive redesigns.

---

# 6. Release Governance

Product releases are governed by the following principles:

* Every release must align with the Product Vision (PF-03).
* Every release must support the Business Model (PF-04).
* No release should violate the Domain Model (PF-02).
* Product scope must remain aligned with business priorities.
* New capabilities should be introduced only after validating customer demand.
* Major architectural changes require review and approval by the Product and Architecture teams.

This governance model ensures consistency across product planning, development, testing, and commercial execution.

# 7. Phase 1 – Foundation Release

## 7.1 Release Objective

Phase 1 establishes the operational foundation of Digitize by OreeOn by delivering a complete, production-ready SaaS platform for swimming pool businesses.

The objective is not to build every possible feature but to create a stable, scalable, and configurable platform that solves the day-to-day operational challenges faced by swimming pool operators.

This release validates the platform architecture, subscription model, licensing framework, and overall customer experience while creating the foundation for future expansion.

---

## 7.2 Business Objectives

The primary business objectives of Phase 1 are:

* Successfully launch the first commercial version of the platform.
* Digitize swimming pool operations end-to-end.
* Validate the Facility-based subscription model.
* Establish recurring subscription revenue.
* Build long-term customer relationships.
* Collect operational feedback from real customers.
* Create a scalable foundation for future Facility Types.

---

## 7.3 Target Customers

# 9. Phase 3 – Enterprise Platform

## 9.1 Release Objective

Phase 3 transforms Digitize by OreeOn from a configurable operational platform into an enterprise-grade Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) platform capable of supporting large organizations operating multiple facilities across different locations.

The focus shifts from managing individual facilities to enabling centralized governance, standardized business operations, enterprise reporting, and organization-wide visibility.

This phase positions OreeOn to serve franchise operators, sports organizations, educational institutions, corporate wellness groups, and enterprises managing multiple activity businesses.

---

## 9.2 Business Objectives

The primary objectives of Phase 3 are:

* Support enterprise-scale organizations.
* Enable centralized governance.
* Standardize operations across facilities.
* Improve executive visibility.
* Simplify enterprise administration.
* Increase customer retention through enterprise capabilities.
* Introduce higher-value subscription offerings.

---

## 9.3 Enterprise Capabilities

Phase 3 introduces enterprise-focused functionality while preserving the Facility-based operational model.

### Multi-Facility Management

* Review consolidated analytics.

---

### Centralized User Administration

Enterprise organizations can:

Enterprise dashboards provide insights across the entire Organization.

# 12. Feature Prioritization Framework

## 12.1 Purpose

As OreeOn evolves, new feature requests will originate from customers, internal teams, partners, and market opportunities.

To maintain product quality and strategic alignment, all features will be evaluated using a standardized prioritization framework.

---

## 12.2 Evaluation Criteria

Every proposed feature should be assessed against the following criteria.

| Evaluation Area      | Key Question                                     |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Strategic Alignment  | Does the feature support the Product Vision?     |
| Customer Value       | Does it solve a meaningful customer problem?     |
| Business Value       | Will it improve revenue, retention, or adoption? |
| Platform Reusability | Can it benefit multiple Facility Types?          |
| Technical Complexity | What is the implementation effort?               |
| Operational Impact   | Does it simplify or complicate operations?       |
| Scalability          | Will it support future platform growth?          |

Features that score highly across these dimensions should receive higher priority.

---

## 12.3 Prioritization Principles

Product prioritization follows these guiding principles:

* Customer value before feature quantity.
* Platform capabilities before customer-specific customizations.
* Configuration before code duplication.
* Long-term maintainability before short-term convenience.
* Business outcomes before technical preferences.
