# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the business model of Digitize by OreeOn Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

While the Product Vision Document explains why the platform exists, this document explains how OreeOn creates value for its customers, delivers that value through a scalable Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) platform, and generates sustainable business revenue.

The Business Model serves as the commercial blueprint for the organization and provides strategic guidance for product management, sales, marketing, customer success, finance, and executive leadership.

It establishes a common understanding of the company's monetization strategy, customer engagement model, licensing approach, subscription framework, growth strategy, and long-term commercial vision.

This document complements the Product Foundation by defining the business operating model that supports the continued evolution of the Digitize by OreeOn platform.

# 2. Executive Summary

Digitize by OreeOn Technologies Pvt. Ltd. is a configurable SaaS platform built to digitalize the operations of activity and recreation businesses.

Unlike traditional software that focuses on a single industry or operational function, OreeOn provides a unified operating platform capable of supporting multiple Facility Types through configuration rather than custom software development.

The initial product release focuses on swimming pools, establishing a robust operational foundation that includes customer management, memberships, attendance, billing, trainer management, reporting, and business administration.

The long-term business strategy is to expand this same platform into additional Facility Types such as gyms, yoga centres, dance studios, sports academies, and wellness businesses while maintaining a single product architecture.

OreeOn follows a recurring subscription business model where organizations subscribe to platform capabilities based on the operational needs of each Facility.

This approach allows businesses to adopt only the capabilities they require while enabling OreeOn to introduce additional commercial offerings as the platform evolves.

The company's growth strategy is built upon three principles:

* Deliver measurable operational value.
* Scale through reusable platform capabilities.
* Grow through long-term customer relationships.

# 3. Business Vision

The long-term business vision of OreeOn is to become the preferred operating platform for activity and recreation businesses across the world.

The OreeOn business model is based on delivering software as a recurring subscription service.

Customers subscribe to the platform, configure it according to their operational requirements, and use it to manage their daily business activities.

# 10. Subscription Model

## 10.1 Subscription Philosophy

Digitize by OreeOn uses a **Facility-based subscription model**.

A subscription is assigned to an individual Facility rather than the entire Organization.

This allows each Facility to subscribe according to its operational maturity, business size, and required platform capabilities.

For example:

| Organization | Facility                 | Subscription Plan |
| ------------ | ------------------------ | ----------------- |
| ABC Sports   | HSR Swimming Pool        | Grow              |
| ABC Sports   | Jayanagar Yoga Center    | Launch            |
| ABC Sports   | Whitefield Gym           | Scale             |

Each Facility operates independently while Organization Administrators continue to receive consolidated reporting and governance across all subscribed Facilities.

This approach provides greater commercial flexibility than organization-wide licensing and avoids requiring smaller facilities to pay for capabilities they do not yet need.

---

## 10.2 Subscription Principles

The subscription model is governed by the following principles:

* Subscription is assigned per Facility.
* Facilities within the same Organization may have different subscription plans.
* Platform capabilities are determined by the assigned License.
* Organizations may upgrade or downgrade individual Facility subscriptions.
* Future enterprise plans will support bundled licensing for multiple Facilities.

This model aligns commercial flexibility with the operational realities of growing organizations.

# 12. Licensing Strategy

## 12.1 Overview

A **Subscription** represents the commercial agreement between the customer and OreeOn.

A **License** represents the technical entitlement that activates platform capabilities.

Separating subscriptions from licenses provides greater flexibility and enables the platform to support future commercial models without architectural changes.

---

# 12.2 Business Relationship

The relationship between subscriptions and licenses is illustrated below.

```text
Subscription
      │
      ▼
License
      │
      ▼
Platform Capabilities
      │
      ▼
User Experience
```

The subscription defines **what the customer has purchased**.

The license defines **what the platform enables**.

---

# 12.3 License Assignment

During Phase 1:

* Each Facility receives one active License.
* The License is linked to the Facility's Subscription.
* Platform capabilities are activated according to the assigned License.

Example:

| Facility                 | Subscription | Assigned License |
| ------------------------ | ------------ | ---------------- |
| HSR Swimming Pool        | Grow         | Grow License     |
| Jayanagar Swimming Pool  | Launch       | Launch License   |
| Whitefield Swimming Pool | Scale        | Scale License    |

Each Facility may operate under a different subscription plan within the same Organization.

---

# 12.4 License Responsibilities

The License determines:

* Available platform modules.
* Enabled business capabilities.
* Operational limits.
* Reporting access.
* Dashboard visibility.
* Administrative permissions.
* Future premium services.

The application dynamically adapts based on the assigned License.
