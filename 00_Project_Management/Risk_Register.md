# 1. Purpose

The Risk Register identifies, assesses, tracks, and manages potential risks that may affect the successful delivery and long-term operation of Digitize by OreeOn Technologies Pvt. Ltd..

The objective is to proactively identify risks before they become issues and define mitigation strategies to minimize their impact.

---

# 2. Risk Management Objectives

The Risk Register helps the project team to:

- Identify project risks early.
- Evaluate business and technical impact.
- Assign ownership.
- Define mitigation strategies.
- Monitor risk status throughout the project lifecycle.

# 5. Risk Response Strategy

Every identified risk should include:

- Description
- Root Cause
- Probability
- Business Impact
- Technical Impact
- Mitigation Plan
- Contingency Plan
- Owner
- Review Frequency

---

# 6. Risk Review Process

Risks should be reviewed:

- During major milestone completion.
- Before development starts.
- Before production deployment.
- During release planning.
- After significant product changes.

---

# 7. Risk Status

Possible statuses:

- Open
- Under Review
- Mitigated
- Closed
- Accepted

---

# 8. Guiding Principles

- Identify risks early.
- Address high-impact risks first.
- Assign ownership for every risk.
- Review risks periodically.
- Document mitigation actions.
