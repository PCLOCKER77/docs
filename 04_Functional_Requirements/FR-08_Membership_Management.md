### Purpose

To create a Membership record that links an eligible Customer to an active Membership Plan and establishes the commercial and operational basis for service entitlement.

### Functional Requirement

The system shall allow authorized Reception Staff to create a Membership for an active Customer by selecting an active Membership Plan, configuring validity terms, validating eligibility, calculating charges, creating required billing references, assigning initial status, recording history, and generating audit events.

### Business Justification

Membership creation is the commercial entry point for customer entitlement. It ensures that attendance, activity participation, billing, payments, notifications, reporting, and customer service all operate against a single governed Membership record.

### Actors

- Reception Staff
- Customer
- Billing Service
- Payment Service
- Notification Service
- Audit Service
- System

### Preconditions

- Organization exists and is Active.
- Customer exists under the same Organization.
- Customer status permits Membership creation.
- Facility exists and is Active.
- Membership Plan exists, belongs to the selected Facility, and is Active.
- Reception Staff is authenticated and authorized.
- Membership pricing, tax, billing, and payment rules are configured.

### Trigger

Reception Staff initiates Membership purchase or creation from Customer Profile, Membership Purchase, or Membership Plan Selection.

### In Scope

- Customer selection.
- Active Membership Plan selection.
- Membership validity configuration.
- Membership type assignment.
- Facility and Site context validation.
- Charge calculation request.
- Initial Membership record creation.
- Initial status assignment.
- Membership History creation.
- Audit and notification event generation.

### Out of Scope

- Permanent deletion of Membership records.
- Subscription Plan configuration.
- Platform licensing.
- Attendance recording.
- Refund processing.
- Cross-Organization Membership transfer.

### Main Flow

1. Reception Staff opens Membership Purchase for an existing Customer.
2. The system loads Customer, Organization, Facility, Site, plan, pricing, and eligibility context.
3. Reception Staff selects an active Membership Plan.
4. The system displays plan benefits, membership type, validity options, pricing, and constraints.
5. Reception Staff confirms Membership terms and payment handling.
6. The system validates Customer status, Organization scope, plan status, Facility ownership, Site applicability, entitlement constraints, and duplicate or multiple membership policy.
7. The system calculates Membership charges and requests billing creation where applicable.
8. The system creates the Membership record with initial status based on payment and activation policy.
9. The system records Membership History and audit events.
10. The system sends configured notifications and displays the Membership creation confirmation.

### Alternate Flows

- If payment is deferred by Organization policy, the Membership is created in Pending Payment status.
- If the Membership is complimentary, payment processing is skipped and activation eligibility is evaluated immediately.
- If multiple active memberships are permitted, the system allows creation after validating plan compatibility.
- If identity or document verification is pending, the Membership may remain Draft or Pending Activation based on policy.

### Exception Flows

- If Customer is inactive, blocked, archived, or outside authorized scope, Membership creation is rejected.
- If the selected Membership Plan is inactive, retired, or not owned by the selected Facility, creation is rejected.
- If mandatory validity dates are missing or invalid, save is blocked.
- If billing calculation fails, Membership creation is not completed unless deferred billing policy permits.
- If notification delivery fails, Membership creation remains valid and the failure is logged.

### Outputs

- Membership record created.
- Membership ID generated.
- Membership status assigned.
- Billing reference created where applicable.
- Membership History entry recorded.
- Business events emitted.
- Audit events recorded.
- Notifications generated.

### UI Behaviour

The Membership Purchase screen shall provide Customer context, plan comparison, validity preview, pricing summary, eligibility warnings, payment requirement indicators, and confirmation review. The Create action shall remain disabled until mandatory fields and validation checks are complete.

### Purpose

To convert an eligible Membership into an Active entitlement after required validations, payment confirmation, and business policy checks are satisfied.

### Functional Requirement

The system shall activate a Membership only after verifying Customer status, Membership Plan status, validity period, payment confirmation or approved deferred payment, Facility/Site eligibility, and downstream entitlement synchronization.

### Business Justification

Activation controls when a Customer is permitted to consume services. Separating activation from creation prevents unauthorized access, incorrect attendance eligibility, and financial leakage.

### Actors

- Reception Staff
- Customer
- Billing Service
- Payment Service
- Attendance Service
- Notification Service
- Audit Service
- System

### Preconditions

- Membership exists in Draft, Pending Payment, or Pending Activation status.
- Customer is eligible for service access.
- Membership Plan is Active.
- Required payment has succeeded or deferred payment policy is approved.
- Validity details are complete.
- Actor or system process has activation authority.

### Trigger

Activation is triggered when payment confirmation is received, deferred payment is approved, complimentary activation is confirmed, or authorized staff selects Activate Membership.

### In Scope

- Activation eligibility validation.
- Payment confirmation check.
- Deferred payment policy check.
- Status update to Active.
- Entitlement synchronization.
- Activation notification.
- Membership History and audit recording.

### Out of Scope

- Payment collection execution.
- Invoice generation.
- Attendance check-in.
- Membership renewal.
- Membership plan configuration.

### Inputs

- Membership ID
- Payment reference, where applicable
- Invoice reference, where applicable
- Activation date
- Deferred payment approval reference, where applicable
- Actor remarks

### Field Definitions

| Field Name | Description | Data Type | Mandatory | Source | Validation | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Membership ID | Membership to activate | Identifier | Yes | User / Event | Must exist and be eligible | Primary input |
| Activation Date | Effective activation date | DateTime | Yes | System / User | Must align with validity policy | Usually current date |
| Payment Reference | Confirmed payment | Identifier | Conditional | Payment Service | Required unless deferred or waived | Links financial proof |
| Deferred Approval Reference | Approval for unpaid activation | Identifier | Conditional | Approval Workflow | Required if deferred activation applies | Policy-driven |
| Activation Status | Resulting status | Enum | Yes | System | Active or rejected | Lifecycle state |

### Main Flow

1. The system receives an activation request or payment confirmation.
2. The system retrieves Membership, Customer, Membership Plan, billing, and payment context.
3. The system validates Customer eligibility, Membership status, plan activity, validity period, and Facility/Site scope.
4. The system validates successful payment or approved deferred payment policy.
5. The system updates Membership status to Active.
6. The system activates service entitlements for attendance, activity participation, and eligible services.
7. The system records activation history.
8. The system emits business and audit events.
9. The system sends activation notifications.
10. The system returns activation confirmation.

### Alternate Flows

- If payment is waived by policy, activation proceeds after non-financial validation.
- If activation is scheduled for a future date, the system stores the activation schedule and activates on the effective date.
- If payment confirmation arrives asynchronously, the system completes activation after reconciling the payment event.

### Exception Flows

- If payment is required but not confirmed, activation is blocked.
- If the Membership Plan is inactive or retired, activation is rejected.
- If Customer status is not eligible, activation is rejected.
- If entitlement synchronization fails, activation is rolled back or marked Pending Activation based on transaction policy.
- If duplicate activation is requested, the system returns the current Active state without creating duplicate history.

### Outputs

- Membership status updated to Active.
- Activation timestamp recorded.
- Entitlements synchronized.
- Membership History updated.
- Audit Log updated.
- Activation notification generated.

### UI Behaviour

The Activate action shall be visible only for eligible Membership states. The UI shall display payment status, validation status, start/end dates, entitlement summary, and confirmation prompts before activation.

### Functional Requirement

### Main Flow

2. The system retrieves Memberships that may be expired based on validity or consumption rules.
3. The system evaluates end date, visit balance, freeze status, grace period, and renewal status.
4. The system excludes Memberships that are renewed, frozen under policy, cancelled, archived, or still in grace period.
5. The system updates eligible Memberships to Expired.
6. The system closes operational entitlements.
7. The system records Membership History for each expired Membership.
8. The system sends expiry notifications where configured.
9. The system records audit events.
10. The system updates reporting datasets and job execution metrics.

### Alternate Flows

- If grace period is configured, Membership may move to Grace Period before Expired.
- If renewal is completed before expiry job execution, the Membership remains Active.
- If Membership is Frozen and policy pauses expiry, the Membership is excluded until resume or adjusted date.

### Exception Flows

- If scheduler fails, operational alert is generated and job can be retried.
- If entitlement closure fails, Membership is marked Expired Pending Synchronization.
- If a Membership has inconsistent validity data, it is excluded and data quality issue is logged.
- If notification fails, expiry remains valid and notification failure is recorded.
