# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the pricing framework for **Digitize**, the Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) platform developed and operated by **OreeOn**.

This document establishes the principles that govern how the platform is commercially positioned, how subscription plans are monetized, and how pricing evolves over time.

It intentionally focuses on the pricing architecture and commercial strategy rather than specific monetary values.

Actual pricing values are managed separately through finance-controlled commercial catalogs and operational pricing systems.

This document serves as the strategic reference for Product Management, Finance, Sales, Customer Success, Engineering, and Business Leadership.

---

# 2. Executive Summary

Digitize follows a value-based pricing strategy designed around the operational structure of customer businesses.

The pricing model is intended to be:

* Predictable
* Transparent
* Scalable
* Fair
* Easy to understand
* Sustainable

Pricing is based on the value delivered to customer organizations rather than technical consumption metrics.

The commercial pricing framework aligns with the subscription architecture defined in **CS-01 – Subscription & Licensing Strategy**.

The primary pricing dimensions are:

* Facility Subscription
* Site Expansion
* Optional Capability Packs
* Enterprise Commercial Agreements

This separation enables OreeOn to evolve pricing without affecting the underlying product architecture.

---

# 3. Pricing Philosophy

Pricing is one of the most important commercial decisions for the Digitize platform.

The objective is not simply to generate revenue, but to create a pricing model that supports long-term customer relationships while sustaining continued product investment.

The pricing strategy is governed by the following principles.

---

## 3.1 Value-Based Pricing

Customers pay for the business value they receive rather than the amount of software they consume.

Digitize is priced according to operational enablement and business capabilities, not infrastructure utilization.

This ensures that customers can confidently grow their businesses without worrying about hidden usage costs.

---

## 3.2 Business-Centric Pricing

Pricing reflects the customer's business structure.

Commercial charges are aligned with operational business units rather than technical entities.

The primary commercial dimensions are:

* Facilities
* Sites
* Commercial Capabilities
* Enterprise Agreements

This approach mirrors how customers naturally think about their organizations.

---

## 3.3 Predictable Commercial Model

Customers should always understand:

* What they are paying for.
* Why they are paying.
* How expansion affects pricing.
* What is included in each subscription plan.
* What additional commercial options are available.

Predictability strengthens customer trust and simplifies commercial discussions.

---

## 3.4 Growth-Friendly Pricing

Digitize encourages customer growth.

Pricing shall never discourage organizations from:

* Registering additional members.
* Expanding attendance.
* Increasing trainers.
* Growing customer databases.
* Operating more efficiently.

Business success should never become a pricing penalty.

---

## 3.5 Sustainable Revenue

The pricing model must provide sufficient recurring revenue to support:

* Continuous product development.
* Infrastructure operations.
* Customer success.
* Technical support.
* Security improvements.
* Innovation.

Pricing should balance customer affordability with long-term platform sustainability.

---

# 4. Pricing Architecture

The pricing architecture defines the commercial components that contribute to the overall subscription value.

```text
Digitize Pricing

        │

        ▼

Facility Subscription

        │

        ▼

Included Commercial Entitlements

        │

        ▼

Additional Site Expansion

        │

        ▼

Optional Capability Packs

        │

        ▼

Enterprise Commercial Agreements
```

Each pricing component serves a distinct commercial purpose.

---

## 4.1 Facility Subscription

The Facility Subscription is the primary commercial component of Digitize.

Each subscribed Facility receives:

* Platform access.
* Subscription plan.
* Commercial entitlement.
* Operational licensing.

The Facility Subscription represents the foundation of recurring revenue.

---

## 4.2 Site Expansion

Organizations may expand Facilities by operating additional Sites.

Version 1.0 assumes:

* By default, Version 1.0 commercial plans include one (1) Site.

The platform architecture supports multiple Sites per Facility.

Additional Sites are enabled through configurable commercial Site Add-ons.
* Additional Sites are commercially managed through Site Add-ons.

The pricing model for Site expansion remains independent from subscription plan pricing.

---

## 4.3 Capability Packs

Future commercial offerings may introduce optional Capability Packs.

Examples include:

* AI Assistant
* Advanced Analytics
* WhatsApp Automation
* Marketing Automation
* Public APIs
* Payroll
* Marketplace Services

Capability Packs allow customers to purchase additional value without changing their core subscription plan.

---

## 4.4 Enterprise Agreements

Enterprise customers may receive customized commercial agreements.

Enterprise pricing may include:

* Consolidated subscriptions.
* Multi-Facility agreements.
* Volume-based pricing.
* Custom commercial terms.
* Premium support.
* Strategic partnership arrangements.

Enterprise pricing continues to follow the principles established in this document while allowing commercial flexibility for large organizations.

---

# 5. Subscription Plan Positioning

## 5.1 Purpose

Subscription plans represent different stages of business maturity rather than different versions of the Digitize platform.

All customers operate on the same core platform developed by OreeOn.

Subscription plans determine the commercial package of business capabilities available to each subscribed Facility.

The objective is to provide organizations with a clear growth path as their operational requirements evolve.

---

## 5.2 Commercial Positioning

Digitize provides four standard subscription plans.

| Plan       | Business Stage                 | Primary Objective               |
| ---------- | ------------------------------ | ------------------------------- |
| Launch     | Start Digital Operations       | Establish a digital foundation  |
| Grow       | Improve Operational Efficiency | Expand business capabilities    |
| Scale      | Optimize Business Performance  | Increase operational excellence |
| Enterprise | Multi-Facility Governance      | Centralized business management |

The plans represent a progression in business maturity while maintaining a consistent user experience.

---

## 5.3 Launch Plan

### Target Customers

The Launch plan is intended for organizations beginning their digital transformation journey.

Typical customer profile:

* Independent swimming pools
* Small gyms
* Yoga studios
* Dance academies
* Newly established facilities

### Commercial Objective

The Launch plan enables organizations to replace manual operations with a structured digital platform.

Typical focus areas include:

* Membership Management
* Attendance Management
* Billing & Invoicing
* Customer Management
* Basic Reporting

The Launch plan provides the essential capabilities required to operate digitally.

---

## 5.4 Grow Plan

### Target Customers

The Grow plan is intended for organizations experiencing business growth and requiring additional operational capabilities.

Typical customer profile:

* Established facilities
* Growing customer base
* Increasing staff
* Multiple operational processes
* Enhanced reporting needs

### Commercial Objective

The Grow plan focuses on improving operational efficiency through additional automation and business intelligence.

Typical capability enhancements may include:

* Advanced Reports
* Enhanced Dashboards
* Marketing Tools
* Advertisement Management
* Operational Analytics
* Extended Configuration Options

---

## 5.5 Scale Plan

### Target Customers

The Scale plan supports organizations with mature operational processes and higher management complexity.

Typical customer profile:

* Large facilities
* High operational volume
* Multiple departments
* Advanced reporting requirements
* Integration needs

### Commercial Objective

The Scale plan is designed to optimize business performance through advanced operational capabilities.

Potential commercial value includes:

* Executive Dashboards
* API Integrations
* Workflow Automation
* Advanced Analytics
* Security Enhancements
* Operational Insights

---

## 5.6 Enterprise Plan

### Target Customers

The Enterprise plan is designed for organizations operating multiple Facilities or requiring centralized governance.

Typical customer profile:

* Corporate organizations
* Franchise operators
* Multi-brand businesses
* Large recreation groups
* Enterprise customers

### Commercial Objective

The Enterprise plan provides centralized governance while maintaining independent Facility operations.

Typical value includes:

* Enterprise Administration
* Organization-wide Reporting
* Centralized Governance
* Enterprise Integrations
* Premium Support
* Dedicated Customer Success

Enterprise commercial agreements may include customized commercial arrangements while remaining compatible with the standard subscription architecture.

# 6. Billing Frequency Strategy

## 6.1 Purpose

Billing frequency defines how often customers are invoiced for their subscriptions.

The billing strategy is designed to provide flexibility while encouraging long-term customer relationships.

---

## 6.2 Supported Billing Cycles

Version 1.0 supports the following billing cycles.

| Billing Cycle | Description                                    |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Monthly       | Subscription renewed every month.              |
| Quarterly     | Subscription renewed every three months.       |
| Half-Yearly   | Subscription renewed every six months.         |
| Annual        | Subscription renewed once every twelve months. |

Additional billing cycles may be introduced through future commercial policy updates.

---

## 6.3 Billing Principles

Billing cycles shall:

* Be selected during subscription purchase.
* Be clearly communicated before payment.
* Apply consistently throughout the subscription period.
* Support renewal according to commercial policy.
* Maintain complete billing history.

---

## 6.4 Billing Cycle Changes

Organizations may request changes to their billing cycle.

Examples include:

* Monthly to Annual
* Quarterly to Annual
* Annual to Quarterly (subject to commercial policy)

Billing cycle changes shall comply with OreeOn's commercial governance and may become effective immediately or at the next renewal period, depending on the applicable policy.

Commercial calculations for billing cycle changes are defined by Finance and implemented through the Billing Engine.

# 7. Site Pricing Strategy

## 7.1 Purpose

The Site Pricing Strategy defines how additional operational locations are commercialized within the Facility-based subscription model.

Sites represent business expansion rather than independent subscriptions.

---

## 7.2 Commercial Principle

The Facility remains the primary subscription unit.

Sites extend the operational reach of the subscribed Facility.

Site pricing is therefore designed as an expansion model rather than a separate subscription model.

---

## 7.3 Version 1.0 Pricing Assumption

Version 1.0 adopts the following pricing assumptions:

* By default, every Facility subscription includes one (1) Site.
* The platform architecture supports multiple Sites per Facility.
* Additional Sites are enabled through configurable commercial Site Add-ons.
* Site Add-ons inherit the subscription plan and enabled capabilities of the parent Facility.
* Site Add-ons are priced per additional Site and billed together with the parent Facility subscription.
* Site pricing remains independent of Capability Packs.

These assumptions may evolve through future commercial strategy revisions.

---

## 7.4 Pricing Objectives

The Site Pricing Strategy is intended to:

* Encourage business expansion.
* Maintain pricing transparency.
* Avoid unnecessary commercial complexity.
* Preserve Facility-centric subscriptions.
* Support future enterprise agreements.

---

## 7.5 Future Site Commercial Models

The pricing architecture has been intentionally designed to support future commercial models without changing the subscription engine.

Potential future models include:

* Site Packs
* Regional Site Bundles
* Unlimited Site Plans
* Enterprise Site Agreements

These models can be introduced as commercial enhancements while preserving backward compatibility.

---

# 8. Enterprise Pricing Strategy

## 8.1 Purpose

The Enterprise Pricing Strategy defines how OreeOn commercially engages with large organizations whose operational, governance, and commercial requirements extend beyond the standard subscription plans.

Enterprise pricing is not a separate product offering. It is a commercial framework built upon the same Digitize platform and subscription architecture defined in CS-01.

The objective is to provide flexibility for large organizations while maintaining a consistent commercial model across all customer segments.

---

## 8.2 Enterprise Pricing Principles

Enterprise pricing shall be governed by the following principles:

* Preserve the Facility-based subscription architecture.
* Support centralized commercial agreements.
* Enable flexible commercial negotiations.
* Maintain transparency in pricing and billing.
* Encourage long-term customer partnerships.
* Avoid creating custom product editions.

Enterprise pricing should simplify commercial management without introducing unnecessary operational complexity.

---

## 8.3 Enterprise Commercial Components

Enterprise agreements may include one or more of the following commercial components:

* Consolidated Facility subscriptions.
* Multi-Site commercial agreements.
* Capability Pack bundles.
* Dedicated onboarding services.
* Premium customer support.
* Integration services.
* Professional services.
* Custom Service Level Agreements (SLAs).
* Strategic account management.

Each component shall be governed by the commercial agreement executed between OreeOn and the customer.

---

## 8.4 Enterprise Commercial Agreements

Enterprise agreements may include customized commercial terms such as:

* Organization-wide billing.
* Consolidated invoicing.
* Unified renewal cycles.
* Volume-based commercial incentives.
* Multi-year agreements.
* Phased implementation plans.

These commercial arrangements shall not alter the underlying subscription or licensing architecture.

---

## 8.5 Enterprise Governance

Enterprise pricing shall remain subject to OreeOn's commercial governance policies.

All customized pricing arrangements must be:

* Approved through defined commercial processes.
* Fully documented.
* Auditable.
* Compatible with Finance and Billing operations.

No Enterprise agreement shall bypass platform licensing controls.

---

# 9. Capability Pack Pricing

## 9.1 Purpose

Capability Packs provide organizations with the ability to extend the value of Digitize by purchasing optional business capabilities without changing their core subscription plan.

Capability Packs support a modular commercial strategy that enables customers to adopt additional functionality as their business evolves.

---

## 9.2 Commercial Philosophy

Capability Packs are optional.

Organizations purchase additional value rather than upgrading their entire subscription solely to access a specific capability.

This approach provides:

* Greater commercial flexibility.
* Reduced barriers to adoption.
* Improved customer choice.
* More efficient monetization of premium functionality.

---

## 9.3 Potential Capability Packs

Future commercial offerings may include:

* Artificial Intelligence (AI) Assistant.
* Advanced Analytics.
* WhatsApp Automation.
* Marketing Automation.
* Payroll Management.
* Public API Access.
* Marketplace Services.
* Advanced Reporting.
* Digital Forms & Workflows.
* Business Intelligence Dashboards.

The availability and composition of Capability Packs shall be governed through future commercial strategy revisions.

---

## 9.4 Capability Pack Principles

Capability Packs shall:

* Operate independently of subscription plans where appropriate.
* Remain compatible with the core subscription architecture.
* Be individually licensable.
* Support future bundling strategies.
* Preserve backward compatibility for existing customers.

Capability Packs provide incremental commercial value while avoiding unnecessary complexity in subscription plan design.

---

## 9.5 Bundling Strategy

OreeOn may introduce bundled commercial offerings that combine multiple Capability Packs.

Examples include:

* Operations Bundle.
* Marketing Bundle.
* Intelligence Bundle.
* Enterprise Bundle.

Bundled offerings should provide simplified purchasing and enhanced customer value while maintaining transparency in commercial positioning.

# 10. Taxes & Regional Pricing

## 10.1 Purpose

The pricing framework shall support operation across multiple regions and tax jurisdictions.

The objective is to ensure that Digitize can expand geographically without requiring changes to the core commercial architecture.

---

## 10.2 Tax Principles

Taxes shall be managed independently of subscription pricing.

Pricing shall distinguish between:

* Base subscription value.
* Applicable taxes.
* Regulatory charges.
* Optional commercial services.

This separation improves transparency and simplifies financial reporting.

---

## 10.3 Regional Pricing

OreeOn may adopt regional pricing strategies based on:

* Country.
* Currency.
* Purchasing power.
* Local market conditions.
* Competitive landscape.
* Regulatory requirements.

Regional pricing shall preserve consistent product positioning while allowing localized commercial flexibility.

---

## 10.4 Currency Support

The commercial platform shall support multiple currencies as part of future international expansion.

Currency management shall include:

* Customer billing currency.
* Invoice currency.
* Reporting currency (where applicable).
* Exchange rate governance.

Currency conversion methodologies shall be defined by Finance.

---

## 10.5 Tax Configuration

The Billing Engine shall support configurable tax policies without requiring software customization.

Tax configuration may include:

* Country-specific tax rules.
* State or provincial taxes.
* Tax exemptions.
* Reverse charge mechanisms (where applicable).
* Future regulatory updates.

The pricing strategy remains independent of specific tax rates, which are maintained through Finance-controlled configurations.

---

# 11. Pricing Governance

## 11.1 Purpose

Pricing Governance establishes the policies, responsibilities, approval processes, and controls that ensure the Digitize pricing strategy remains consistent, sustainable, and aligned with OreeOn's long-term business objectives.

The objective of Pricing Governance is to balance commercial flexibility with operational stability while protecting customer trust and business profitability.

---

## 11.2 Governance Principles

Pricing decisions shall follow these principles:

* Consistency across customer segments.
* Transparency in commercial communication.
* Controlled approval processes.
* Clear accountability.
* Financial sustainability.
* Compliance with applicable regulations.

Pricing changes shall never compromise customer confidence or create unnecessary operational complexity.

---

## 11.3 Pricing Ownership

Pricing is a cross-functional responsibility.

| Function            | Responsibility                                                                                                           |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Business Leadership | Defines overall commercial direction and approves strategic pricing changes.                                             |
| Product Management  | Ensures pricing aligns with product positioning and capability packaging.                                                |
| Finance             | Defines pricing policies, taxation, revenue recognition, and financial compliance.                                       |
| Sales               | Provides market feedback and executes approved pricing.                                                                  |
| Customer Success    | Provides customer adoption insights and renewal feedback.                                                                |
| Engineering         | Implements pricing rules through configurable platform services without embedding commercial values in application code. |

No single department should independently modify the commercial pricing model.

---

## 11.4 Pricing Change Management

Pricing changes shall follow a controlled governance process.

Typical pricing changes include:

* Introduction of new subscription plans.
* Modification of plan positioning.
* New Capability Packs.
* Regional pricing adjustments.
* Enterprise commercial policies.
* Promotional pricing initiatives.

Every pricing change shall be:

* Documented.
* Reviewed.
* Approved.
* Version controlled.
* Communicated before becoming effective.

---

## 11.5 Effective Date Policy

Every pricing change shall include:

* Approval date.
* Effective date.
* Impact assessment.
* Customer communication plan.
* Transition policy for existing customers.

This ensures predictable pricing transitions while minimizing customer disruption.

Default governance: Unless otherwise specified in an approved Pricing Change Request, the governance defaults referenced in the Commercial Governance Appendix (including invoice numbering conventions and approval thresholds) take effect on `2026-08-01` for Version 1.0 baseline behaviors. Finance must confirm region-specific invoice requirements and publish currency-equivalent thresholds where applicable.

# 12. Future Pricing Evolution

## 12.1 Purpose

The pricing architecture has been intentionally designed to evolve as Digitize expands into new industries, markets, and commercial models.

Future enhancements shall extend the existing pricing framework rather than replace it.

---

## 12.2 Evolution Principles

Future pricing shall:

* Preserve the Facility-based commercial model.
* Maintain backward compatibility where practical.
* Avoid unnecessary pricing complexity.
* Align with the Product Vision.
* Support global expansion.

Commercial evolution should always enhance customer value while maintaining architectural consistency.

---

## 12.3 Potential Future Enhancements

The pricing framework is expected to support future initiatives such as:

* AI-powered commercial offerings.
* Industry-specific pricing packages.
* Marketplace revenue sharing.
* Usage-based value-added services that are optional and independent of the core Facility subscription model (for example, SMS delivery, WhatsApp messaging, AI token consumption, or third-party service usage).

* Premium implementation services.
* Managed service offerings.
* White-label commercial agreements.
* Partner and reseller pricing programs.

These enhancements shall integrate with the existing pricing architecture rather than introducing separate commercial models.

---

## 12.4 Pricing Review Cycle

OreeOn should conduct periodic pricing reviews to ensure continued alignment with:

* Market conditions.
* Customer expectations.
* Product capabilities.
* Competitive landscape.
* Financial objectives.
* Regulatory requirements.

Pricing reviews do not necessarily result in pricing changes but provide structured opportunities for strategic evaluation.

# 13. Business Rules Summary

The following business rules establish the pricing baseline for Digitize Version 1.0.

| Rule ID   | Business Rule                                                                                                                  |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| CS-PR-001 | Pricing is based on business value rather than software consumption.                                                           |
| CS-PR-002 | The Facility is the primary pricing unit.                                                                                      |
| CS-PR-003 | By default, Version 1.0 commercial plans include one (1) Site; the platform architecture supports multiple Sites per Facility, and additional Sites are enabled through configurable commercial Site Add-ons. |
| CS-PR-004 | Additional Sites are commercialized through Site Add-ons.                                                                      |
| CS-PR-005 | Subscription plans are differentiated by business capabilities and business maturity, not by user count or transaction limits. |
| CS-PR-006 | Capability Packs are optional commercial offerings independent of the core subscription plan.                                  |
| CS-PR-007 | Enterprise pricing extends the standard commercial framework and does not represent a separate product.                        |
| CS-PR-008 | Taxes are calculated independently from subscription pricing.                                                                  |
| CS-PR-009 | Regional pricing may vary while maintaining consistent product positioning.                                                    |
| CS-PR-010 | Pricing values shall be maintained outside product documentation and application source code.                                  |
| CS-PR-011 | All pricing changes require formal governance, approval, and version control.                                                  |
| CS-PR-012 | Pricing architecture shall evolve through configuration rather than software redesign.                                         |

These business rules establish the commercial pricing governance for Digitize Version 1.0.
