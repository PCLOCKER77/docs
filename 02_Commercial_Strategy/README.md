# Commercial Strategy

## Overview

The **Commercial Strategy** documentation defines how **OreeOn Technologies Pvt. Ltd.** commercializes **Digitize** as a Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) platform.

This documentation establishes the policies, principles, governance, and operational framework for subscriptions, pricing, billing, invoicing, commercial incentives, and revenue management.

It serves as the authoritative reference for Business Leadership, Product Management, Finance, Sales, Marketing, Customer Success, Engineering, and other stakeholders involved in the commercial lifecycle of Digitize.

The Commercial Strategy is intentionally separated from Business Analysis and Functional Requirements so that commercial decisions can evolve independently while maintaining architectural consistency.

---

# Objectives

The objectives of the Commercial Strategy are to:

* Define the commercial architecture of Digitize.
* Establish subscription and licensing principles.
* Standardize pricing governance.
* Define billing and invoicing processes.
* Govern commercial offers and incentive programs.
* Support scalable revenue operations.
* Enable future international expansion.
* Maintain financial transparency and auditability.

# Guiding Principles

The Commercial Strategy is based on the following principles:

* Facility-based subscription architecture.
* Transparent and value-based pricing.
* Configurable commercial policies.
* Separation of pricing and billing.
* Strong financial governance.
* Complete auditability.
* Customer-centric commercial experience.
* Scalability for global expansion.
