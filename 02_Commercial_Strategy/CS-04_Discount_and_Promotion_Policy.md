# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define how OreeOn creates, manages, governs, and evaluates commercial offers and incentive programs for the Digitize platform.

This document establishes the policies that govern discounts, promotional campaigns, referral incentives, corporate offers, seasonal campaigns, renewal benefits, and future commercial initiatives.

It complements:

* **CS-01 – Subscription & Licensing Strategy**
* **CS-02 – Pricing Strategy**
* **CS-03 – Billing & Invoicing Strategy**

while intentionally avoiding duplication of subscription, pricing, and billing rules.

---

# 2. Executive Summary

Commercial offers are designed to support customer acquisition, retention, expansion, and long-term business relationships.

Digitize adopts a controlled and configurable incentive model where promotional benefits are governed through commercial policies rather than embedded within application logic.

The strategy ensures that:

* Promotions remain transparent.
* Discounts are controlled.
* Financial integrity is preserved.
* Customer trust is maintained.
* Commercial decisions remain auditable.

This document provides the governance framework for all non-standard commercial pricing initiatives.

---

# 3. Commercial Philosophy

Commercial incentives should encourage customer growth without reducing the long-term value of the Digitize platform.

Every offer introduced by OreeOn shall support one or more strategic business objectives such as:

* Customer acquisition.
* Customer retention.
* Expansion into new markets.
* Product adoption.
* Upselling premium capabilities.
* Strengthening long-term customer relationships.

Offers are intended to create value for both the customer and OreeOn.

---

## 3.1 Guiding Principles

Commercial incentives shall follow these principles:

* Fairness.
* Transparency.
* Consistency.
* Auditability.
* Configurability.
* Financial sustainability.

Offers shall never undermine the standard pricing architecture defined in CS-02.

---

## 3.2 Pricing Integrity

The standard pricing strategy remains the baseline commercial model.

Commercial offers represent temporary or conditional adjustments and shall not redefine the permanent pricing structure.

All incentives shall reference the applicable standard pricing before adjustments are applied.

Version 1.0 recognizes the following categories of commercial incentives.

| Renewal Incentives | Encourage continued subscription renewal.                     |

Each category is governed independently while remaining compatible with the commercial architecture established in CS-01, CS-02, and CS-03.

# 5. Discount Strategy

## 5.1 Purpose

Discounts provide controlled reductions to standard pricing under approved commercial conditions.

Discounts are intended to support strategic business objectives while preserving pricing integrity.

---

## 5.2 Discount Principles

Discounts shall:

* Be formally approved.
* Have defined eligibility.
* Have a defined validity period.
* Be fully auditable.
* Never permanently alter standard pricing.

Discounts are commercial exceptions rather than permanent pricing rules.

---

## 5.3 Discount Types

Version 1.0 supports the following conceptual discount types:

* Launch Offers.
* Early Adopter Discounts.
* Volume Discounts.
* Educational Institution Discounts.
* Non-Profit Organization Discounts.
* Promotional Discounts.
* Renewal Discounts.
* Upgrade Discounts.

The availability of each discount type shall be determined by commercial policy.

# 6. Promotion Strategy

## 6.1 Purpose

The Promotion Strategy defines how OreeOn designs, executes, and governs promotional campaigns for the Digitize platform.

Promotions are temporary commercial initiatives intended to achieve specific business objectives while preserving the long-term integrity of the standard pricing model.

Promotions are configurable commercial events and shall not permanently modify subscription pricing.

---

## 6.2 Promotion Objectives

Promotional campaigns may be introduced to support one or more of the following objectives:

* Customer acquisition.
* Product awareness.
* New market expansion.
* Facility onboarding.
* New feature adoption.
* Seasonal demand generation.
* Customer reactivation.
* Business partnerships.
* Strategic sales initiatives.

Every promotion shall have a clearly defined business objective before activation.

---

## 6.3 Promotion Types

Version 1.0 recognizes the following promotional categories:

| Promotion Type            | Description                                                |
| ------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Launch Campaign           | Introduces Digitize into a new market or region.           |
| Limited-Time Offer        | Available only for a defined promotional period.           |
| Early Adopter Campaign    | Rewards early customers during product growth.             |
| Feature Adoption Campaign | Encourages adoption of newly released capabilities.        |
| Seasonal Promotion        | Linked to business or festive seasons.                     |
| Upgrade Promotion         | Encourages customers to move to higher subscription plans. |
| Renewal Promotion         | Encourages subscription renewals before expiry.            |
| Reactivation Campaign     | Encourages inactive customers to return.                   |

Additional campaign types may be introduced through future commercial policy updates.

---

## 6.4 Promotion Configuration

Every promotional campaign shall define:

* Campaign name.
* Campaign objective.
* Applicable products.
* Eligible subscription plans.
* Eligible customer segments.
* Start date.
* End date.
* Applicable incentive.
* Approval reference.
* Campaign owner.

Promotion configuration shall remain data-driven and configurable.

---

```text

   │
   ▼

   │
   ▼

   │
```

Each lifecycle transition shall be recorded for audit purposes.

# 7. Coupon Management

## 7.1 Purpose

Coupon Management provides a controlled mechanism for customers to redeem approved promotional benefits during eligible commercial transactions.

Coupons simplify campaign execution while ensuring financial governance.

---

## 7.2 Coupon Principles

Coupons shall:

* Be uniquely identifiable.
* Have defined validity periods.
* Support configurable eligibility rules.
* Be traceable to the originating campaign.
* Be governed by commercial approval.

Coupons shall never bypass pricing or billing controls.

---

## 7.3 Coupon Attributes

Every coupon should support:

* Coupon code.
* Campaign reference.
* Validity period.
* Usage limit.
* Customer eligibility.
* Subscription plan eligibility.
* Facility eligibility.
* Redemption history.
* Status.

The platform shall support configuration without requiring software modifications.

---

## 7.4 Coupon Lifecycle

```text
Created
   │
   ▼
Published
   │
   ▼
Redeemed
   │
   ▼
Expired
```

Invalid or expired coupons shall not be accepted by the Billing Engine.

# 8. Referral Programs

## 8.1 Purpose

Referral Programs encourage existing customers, partners, or approved participants to introduce new organizations to Digitize.

Referral initiatives support sustainable customer acquisition while rewarding successful advocacy.

---

## 8.2 Referral Principles

Referral programs shall:

* Reward verified referrals only.
* Prevent duplicate claims.
* Maintain complete referral history.
* Operate through configurable business rules.
* Support future expansion.

Referral rewards shall only be issued after predefined commercial conditions are satisfied.

---

## 8.3 Referral Participants

Future referral programs may include:

* Existing customers.
* Business partners.
* Sales representatives.
* Industry consultants.
* Franchise operators.
* Approved affiliates.

Participation shall be governed through commercial policy.

---

## 8.4 Referral Rewards

Future referral incentives may include:

* Subscription discounts.
* Capability Pack credits.
* Service credits.
* Partner commissions.
* Promotional rewards.

Reward types shall remain configurable and independent of the billing architecture.

* Campaign objective.

* End date.

Retention initiatives shall:

# 12. Governance & Approval

## 12.1 Purpose

Commercial offers require structured governance to ensure consistency, transparency, financial control, and regulatory compliance.

No promotional initiative shall be introduced without appropriate business approval.

---

## 12.2 Governance Principles

Commercial incentives shall be:

* Documented.
* Approved.
* Auditable.
* Time-bound.
* Financially controlled.
* Configurable.

All commercial activities shall comply with the pricing principles defined in CS-02 and the billing controls defined in CS-03.

---

## 12.3 Approval Responsibilities

| Function            | Responsibility                                                                             |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Business Leadership | Approves strategic commercial campaigns.                                                   |
| Sales               | Proposes commercial initiatives based on market requirements.                              |
| Marketing           | Designs promotional campaigns and communication strategies.                                |
| Finance             | Reviews financial impact and compliance.                                                   |
| Product Management  | Ensures alignment with product positioning.                                                |
| Engineering         | Implements approved configuration changes without embedding campaign logic in source code. |

Approval workflows shall be documented and retained for audit purposes.

Note: The detailed approval thresholds, campaign budget caps, refund thresholds, invoice numbering conventions, and the default governance effective date are defined in the Commercial Governance Appendix (`CS-Governance_Appendix.md`). Default thresholds include promotional discount bands (Small: ≤20% / ≤1,000 USD; Medium: >20%–≤50% / 1,001–10,000 USD; Large: >50% / >10,000 USD) and a campaign budget cap requiring Platform Owner notification at 50,000 USD. The default governance effective date is 2026-08-01.

---

## 12.4 Campaign Governance

Every commercial initiative shall define:

* Campaign owner.
* Approval authority.
* Effective period.
* Success metrics.
* Closure criteria.

Expired campaigns shall be archived while maintaining complete historical records.

Future enhancements shall extend the existing commercial framework while maintaining compatibility with the established pricing and billing architecture.

# 14. Business Rules Summary

The following business rules establish the governance framework for commercial offers and incentives.

| Rule ID   | Business Rule                                                                           |
| --------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| CS-CO-001 | Standard pricing remains the baseline commercial model.                                 |
| CS-CO-002 | Commercial offers are temporary and governed independently from pricing.                |
| CS-CO-003 | Every promotion shall have defined eligibility and validity periods.                    |
| CS-CO-004 | Coupon usage shall be fully traceable.                                                  |
| CS-CO-005 | Referral rewards shall only be issued after successful qualification.                   |
| CS-CO-006 | Corporate offers require formal commercial approval.                                    |
| CS-CO-007 | Upgrade and renewal incentives shall support long-term customer retention.              |
| CS-CO-008 | Campaigns shall be configurable without software modification.                          |
| CS-CO-009 | Every commercial initiative shall maintain complete audit history.                      |
| CS-CO-010 | Campaign performance shall be measurable and reviewable.                                |
| CS-CO-011 | Expired campaigns shall be archived rather than deleted.                                |
| CS-CO-012 | Commercial incentives shall never bypass pricing, billing, taxation, or audit controls. |
